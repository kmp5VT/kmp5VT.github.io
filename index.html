Karl Pierce

I am a Research Scientist with a bacgkround in
  • Linear and Multilinear Algebra
  • High Performance Computing
  • Tensor Networks and Decompositions
  • Randomized Linear Algebra
  • Accurate Quantum Chemistry
  • Software and Library Design

Previously I have worked as a postdoctoral Associate at the University of Maryland in the Department of Mathematics with Dr. Haizhao Yang studying effective application of tensor algebra scientific machine learning.

Previously, I worked with Dr. Miles Stoudenmire and Dr. Matthew Fishman at the Flatiron Institute's Center for Computational Quantum Physics as a principle developer on the ITensors.jl library. During this time I redesigned the library using Julia style, generic programming principles to support CPU/GPU agnostic algorithm design.

Prior to this experience, I worked with Dr. Edward Valeev to recieve my PhD in theoretical chemistry. During this time, I algorithms to compute the canonical polyadic tensor decomposition and designed high performance tensor kernels to accelerate accurate quantum chemistry modeling software.

Outside of science, you can find me riding my bike or baking sourdough. You might also find me hanging upside-down, I am a pole vaulter and have also coached young athletes in the sport.
